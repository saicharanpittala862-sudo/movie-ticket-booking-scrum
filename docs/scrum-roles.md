
# Scrum Roles and Responsibilities

## Member 1 – Product Owner
- Understands user requirements.
- Prioritizes the Product Backlog.
- Reviews the completed features.

## Member 2 – Scrum Master
- Coordinates Scrum activities.
- Helps resolve team obstacles.
- Encourages communication and collaboration.

## Member 3 – Development Team Member
- Prepares payment requirements.
- Defines payment testing scenarios.
- Helps integrate and test the project.

## Member 4 – Development Team Member
- Prepares E-ticket requirements.
- Integrates the assignment documents.
- Coordinates final report preparation.

## Team Collaboration
All four members participate in sprint planning, reviews, testing discussions, and retrospectives.
