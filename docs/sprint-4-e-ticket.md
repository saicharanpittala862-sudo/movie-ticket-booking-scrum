Sprint 4: E-Ticket Generation

Sprint Goal

Provide an electronic ticket after successful booking and integrate the complete movie booking workflow.

Sprint Backlog

1. Generate a unique booking ID for every confirmed booking.
2. Display the movie name and theatre details.
3. Display the show date, time, and selected seat numbers.
4. Generate a QR code or verification code for ticket validation.
5. Review and integrate the complete booking workflow.
6. Verify that ticket details match the confirmed booking information.
7. Test ticket generation for successful and failed booking scenarios.

Expected Increment

An electronic ticket containing the unique booking ID, movie name, theatre, show date, show time, selected seat numbers, and a QR code or verification code.

Acceptance Criteria

- A ticket is generated only after the booking is confirmed.
- The ticket displays the correct movie and theatre details.
- The show date, time, and seat numbers match the booking.
- Every confirmed booking has a unique booking reference.
- The QR code or verification code supports ticket verification.
- Failed or cancelled bookings do not generate valid confirmed tickets.

Sprint Review

Review the complete end-to-end booking workflow, from movie selection and seat selection to payment confirmation and e-ticket generation.

Verify that booking IDs are unique, ticket information is accurate, and QR code or verification code generation works as expected.

Collect feedback from all team members and document any issues or improvements required for final integration.

Member 4 – E-Ticket and Final Integration

- Update "docs/sprint-4-e-ticket.md" with booking ID, ticket details, and QR code requirements.
- Review "docs/sprint-reviews.md" and incorporate the Sprint 4 review.
- Combine feedback from all team members in "docs/retrospective.md".
- Verify the integration requirements for movie listings, seat selection, payment, and e-ticket generation.
- Commit and push the changes to GitHub.
