# Sprint 3: Payment Workflow

## Sprint Goal
To enable users to pay for their selected movie tickets and receive booking confirmation after successful payment.

## Sprint Backlog
1. Display the selected movie and show timing.
2. Display the selected seats and total amount.
3. Provide a secure payment process.
4. Handle successful and failed payments.
5. Verify payment status before confirming the booking.
6. Test payment scenarios.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The correct ticket price is displayed.
- Successful payments result in booking confirmation.
- Failed payments do not result in confirmed bookings.
- Duplicate payment requests are handled safely.
- Payment status is verified before an E-ticket is generated.

## Expected Increment
A payment workflow that allows users to pay for tickets and receive booking confirmation after successful payment.

## Testing Scenarios
- Test successful payment.
- Test failed payment.
- Test payment timeout.
- Test an incorrect payment status.
- Verify that an E-ticket is not issued before payment confirmation.

## Sprint Review
The team reviews the payment workflow, discusses test results, and records feedback for improvement.
