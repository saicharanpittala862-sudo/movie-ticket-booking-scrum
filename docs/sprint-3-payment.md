
# Sprint 3: Payment

## Sprint Goal
Allow users to pay for their selected movie tickets.

## Sprint Backlog
1. Display the booking summary.
2. Calculate the total payable amount.
3. Plan payment gateway integration.
4. Handle successful and failed payments.
5. Confirm bookings only after verified payment success.

## Expected Increment
A payment workflow that handles payment status and booking confirmation.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The payable amount is displayed.
- Payment success and failure are handled.
- A booking is confirmed only after successful payment verification.

## Sprint Review
Review the planned payment workflow and discuss any issues.
