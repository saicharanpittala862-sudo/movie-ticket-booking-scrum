
# Sprint 4: E-Ticket Generation

## Sprint Goal
Provide an electronic ticket after successful booking.

## Sprint Backlog
1. Generate a unique booking ID.
2. Display the movie name and theatre.
3. Display show date, time, and seat numbers.
4. Generate a QR code or verification code.
5. Review the complete booking workflow.

## Expected Increment
An E-ticket containing the booking ID, movie details, show timing, and seat numbers.

## Acceptance Criteria
- A ticket is generated only after confirmed booking.
- The ticket displays the correct booking details.
- The ticket has a unique booking reference.

## Sprint Review
Review the planned end-to-end booking flow and ticket information.
